## SEO and AI SEO — different mechanics, same goal

**SEO (Search Engine Optimization)** is the work of getting your site ranked high on Google and other search engines. It's built on keywords, quality content, a backlink profile, and the site's technical health (speed, mobile responsiveness, structure). The result: a user searches, sees your site among 10 links, and clicks through.

**AI SEO** isn't one single service — it's the combination of two approaches:

- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** — structuring content so ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and voice search pick it as the direct answer. This works through FAQ structure, a clear question-and-answer format, and schema markup.
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** — strengthening your brand's presence across the web so ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity mention and recommend you specifically: consistent facts, trustworthy sources, and a clearly defined entity (company name, service, location).

Put simply: **SEO means being on Google's list. AI SEO means being named inside the AI's answer.** Both matter, but they work through different mechanisms.

## How SEO and AI SEO differ — comparison table

| Criterion | SEO | AI SEO (AEO/GEO) |

Both rest on the same foundation — accurate, high-quality content and a technically sound site. What differs is the layer on top: SEO optimizes for Google's algorithm, AI SEO optimizes for how an AI model decides which source to cite.

## Why SEO alone isn't enough anymore

The old logic was simple: rank #1 on Google, get the traffic. That formula no longer holds fully. When an AI Overview appears for your query, the user sees the answer at the top and, according to the data, skips the organic results below it in nearly half of cases.

That means **ranking #1 is no longer enough — you need to be inside the AI's answer itself.** And that second spot doesn't come automatically with classic SEO. What content an AI chooses to cite follows different logic: clear paragraphs with a direct answer, structured data (tables, lists, schema markup), and consistent facts about your brand repeated across the web (an entity signal).

In other words: SEO puts you on the list, AI SEO puts you in the answer. Without both, your business stays invisible across half of today's search landscape.

## How AI SEO works — 4 core components

1. **Entity accuracy.** Your company name, address, services, and prices need to be consistent and clear everywhere online — your site, directories, social profiles. If an AI sees conflicting information, it won't treat you as a reliable source.
2. **Structured data (schema markup).** Plain text like "SEO services in Tashkent from $300/month" isn't something an AI reads as a fact. With schema markup, the price, currency, and service type are explicitly tagged, so the AI can drop that straight into an answer.
3. **Question-and-answer formatted content.** AI models "understand" and cite short paragraphs that answer a specific question directly better than long, general text. Opening each section with a direct answer to its own question is more effective than building up to it.
4. **A site open to AI bots.** ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI crawlers need to be able to read your site — a blocked site never appears in an AI answer, no matter how good the content is.

## Combining SEO and AI SEO — a 5-step strategy

### Step 1: Check your technical foundation

A slow, non-mobile-friendly, or broken site won't rank on Google or appear in AI answers. Both directions need the same technical base: fast loading, HTTPS, a clean site structure.

### Step 2: Research keywords and question-intent

Alongside classic keywords, study how users phrase full questions to an AI — queries like "what's the best X" or "how much does Y cost." This is the bridge between the two types of search.

### Step 3: Structure your content

Open each major section with an H2 heading phrased as a question, and answer it directly in 1–2 sentences right below. Tables and lists are the easiest format for both AI models and human readers to parse.

### Step 4: Strengthen entity and trust signals

Company information — name, address, services, prices — should match across your site, social profiles, and directory listings. That's a trust signal for both Google and AI models.

### Step 5: Track results on both fronts

For SEO, monitor rankings and traffic through Google Search Console. For AI SEO, periodically ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini questions relevant to your niche and check whether your brand shows up in the answer.

## FAQ: common questions about SEO and AI SEO

**1. Do I have to do SEO and AI SEO at the same time?**
Not strictly, but it's recommended. SEO captures traffic from today's Google searches, while AI SEO claims a spot in a fast-growing channel. Both rest on the same technical foundation, so running them together is cheaper and more effective than doing them separately.

**2. When do AI SEO results show up?**
The technical layer (schema, site structure) gets indexed by AI crawlers within 2–4 weeks. Brand mentions in AI answers typically start appearing after 1–3 months. Stable, consistent results take 6–12 months, since AI models refresh their knowledge base periodically.

**3. Does a small business even need AI SEO, or is it only for large companies?**
The opposite — AI SEO is especially useful for specialists, consultants, and local service providers. When users ask an AI "a specialist for X near me" or "the best Y service," a small business with clear, structured information gets mentioned more often.

**4. Do I need to pay Google separately to appear in AI Overviews?**
No. Google AI Overviews are generated automatically from organic results — it's not paid advertising. To appear there, your site needs to be accurate, high-quality, and well-structured, just like for organic SEO.

**5. What does a site need to look like for a brand to show up in ChatGPT?**
The site needs to be open to AI bots, provide clear and consistent information, use a question-and-answer structure, and carry schema markup. Beyond that, your brand's facts need to be consistent across other trustworthy sites too — that's the core work of GEO.
