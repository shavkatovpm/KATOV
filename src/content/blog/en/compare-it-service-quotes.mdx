**Why do IT service prices vary so much?** Often, the same label covers different work. “Website development” may mean assembling pages from supplied materials, or it may also include planning the customer journey, editing copy, connecting enquiry forms and testing the result. Experience, availability and working methods also affect the price.

For a business in Uzbekistan commissioning a website, Telegram bot or CRM, the useful question is: **“Exactly what result will this payment buy?”** This guide explains how to compare proposals on equal terms. It is not a market price list. All examples are illustrative, not KATOV tariffs or client case studies.

## Why does the same brief produce different quotes?

“We need a five-page website” specifies page count, but leaves much of the work undefined. Who writes the copy? Is a second language needed? Where should enquiries go? Can the administrator change a service price without a developer?

Quote A might cover one language and customer-supplied materials. Quote B might include two languages, copy editing and enquiry delivery to an administrator. The difference in their totals does not yet tell you which is better value. First decide which tasks you actually need.

The same principle applies to bots and CRM systems. A bot with predefined replies differs from one that passes orders to an operator. Creating customer records differs from migrating an old database and training staff.

## A table for comparing IT service proposals

Ask each provider to mark every item as **included**, **charged separately** or **excluded**. Do not assume an unspecified task is free.

| Item | What the proposal should state |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Number of pages, languages, user roles and features |
| Materials | Who supplies copy, translations, photographs and product data |
| Design | Adaptation of an existing design or a separate design process; revision rounds |
| Integrations | Exactly which data goes to Telegram, a CRM or a payment service |
| Testing | Mobile use, enquiry delivery and error handling |
| Search preparation | Specific title, description, indexing and content tasks |
| Handover | Access, agreed project files and operating instructions |
| Support | Duration, response time and the boundary between defects and new requests |
| Additional fees | Domain, hosting, subscriptions and external services |

[Business Victoria’s guide](https://business.vic.gov.au/business-information/ecommerce/build-a-website/know-what-to-ask-your-web-developer) recommends asking about ongoing costs, support charges and the conditions for moving a website to another provider. It is a source of useful procurement questions, not evidence of Uzbekistan market prices.

## Separate the initial price from the first-year cost

**First-year cost = development + essential launch extras + 12 months of recurring services + annual fees.**

Avoid counting a cost again if it is already included in a package. For quotes in different currencies, use the same comparison date and exchange rate. Clarify the final payable amount, including any taxes specified in the proposal.

The figures below are a hypothetical calculation, not market rates:

| Cost | Quote A | Quote B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Development | UZS 4,000,000 | UZS 6,000,000 |
| Essential content and integrations | UZS 2,000,000 extra | Included |
| Monthly services | UZS 200,000 × 12 | UZS 100,000 × 12 |
| Domain and annual services | UZS 300,000 | UZS 300,000 |
| First-year total | **UZS 8,700,000** | **UZS 7,500,000** |

Here, the quote with the lower starting price costs more over the first year. That is not a universal rule. Monthly services must also be comparable: a higher fee may cover more work that you need.

## Make “finished” something you can test

Agree an acceptance criterion alongside the price. For example: “On a phone, a visitor selects a service and submits a name and number. The designated administrator receives the enquiry, and the visitor sees confirmation. An empty required field produces a clear message.”

That describes an observable result. “A modern, high-quality website” leaves room for conflicting interpretations. State who tests the result and when discovered issues will be corrected. Our [guide to website speed](/en/blog/site-speed-and-seo) covers a separate aspect of performance evaluation.

Clarify broad items such as “SEO included” as well. Initial technical setup differs from recurring content production, analysis and improvements. Ask for the work to be delivered rather than relying on a general promise.

## What can you reduce when the budget is limited?

Preserve the main customer action: enquiry, order, reservation or contact with an operator. Separate features needed at launch from those that can follow later. Fewer pages and one language may suit an initial release if they match your audience.

An existing design can also be a sensible choice when it meets the requirements. The reduced scope should be explicit in the quote. Removing enquiry delivery, necessary access controls or testing of agreed functions simply pushes problems into later stages.

## A brief you can send to providers

> We need a [website/bot/CRM]. The main user is [who], and their main action is [what]. The first release needs [features]. Materials will be prepared by [who]. Please itemise scope, deliverables, timing, testing, exclusions and recurring first-year costs. Explain how price and timing will be agreed if we add a new task.

Send the same brief to every provider, clarify gaps in their answers, and then compare totals. For the wider commissioning process, see our [guide to ordering a website in Tashkent](/en/blog/order-website-tashkent).

## Frequently asked questions

### Why do IT service prices vary?

Quotes may include different amounts of design, content, integrations, testing and support. Experience, availability and the way a provider organises work also affect pricing.

### How should I compare two website quotes?

Send both providers the same brief. Ask them to mark each task as included, charged separately or excluded, then calculate the first-year cost.

### Can I choose the cheapest quote?

Yes, if it covers the necessary work and has clear acceptance criteria. A low price alone does not prove poor quality, and a high price does not guarantee quality.

### What does “SEO included” mean?

Ask for the specific pages, technical settings, content tasks and ongoing analysis covered. A promise about search positions is not a substitute for a list of deliverables.
