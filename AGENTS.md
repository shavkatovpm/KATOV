# KATOV project instructions

## Social content and blog workflow

When the user asks to write a social-media blog/post for KATOV, says “ijtimoiy tarmoq uchun blog yozamiz”, or asks to continue the content plan:

1. Read `docs/social-content-design-brief.md` completely before doing any work.
2. Treat that document as the source of truth for topics, non-repetition, content rules, approved design directions, muted color feedback, bot workflow, website article workflow, and SEO featured-image requirements.
3. Inspect `smm-bot/data/queue.json` and existing `src/content/blog/**` files before choosing a topic. Never repeat an already written or queued topic.
4. Prepare the social post, three distinct bot design options, their matching Instagram versions, the full website article in all three languages (uz/ru/en — none may be skipped or deferred), and its SEO featured image as one continuous workflow. Do not wait for the user’s social-image selection before writing the website article.
5. Choose the article title/angle so it carries strong intent for both classic SEO (a real, searched keyword or phrase — not just a relatable angle) and GEO/AEO (a direct question an AI answer engine would cite). Do not trade one off for the other.
6. Do not publish, deploy, or push unless the user asks for that external action.
